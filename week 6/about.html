About

I got into computers early — how they work, how they're programmed, how they turn into solutions — and that curiosity led me to the Faculty of Computers and Information at Damietta University (2022–2026), specializing in AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning.

Since then I've worked hands-on with real-world datasets: predictive models, classification systems, computer-vision pipelines, and now ADAII (my graduation project on adaptive AI monitoring for athletes) and a live ML deployment at Cellula Technologies. I'm most interested in the part most student projects skip — getting a model out of the notebook and into something that actually runs.
