ADAII — An Adaptive Data-Driven AI Monitoring in Athletes

Graduation project. Wearable IoT, computer vision, and AI for real-time football player performance and injury-risk monitoring.

The problem

Injury in athletes is usually diagnosed after it happens, not before. Coaches and medical staff act on visible symptoms, by which point the damage is already done. ADAII asks whether physiological signal data, captured continuously through wearables, can surface injury risk early enough to intervene.

Approach

The core of the system is Vest Model v8, a Conv2D + GRU architecture trained on the WESAD physiological signal dataset, paired with computer vision for movement analysis. The convolutional layers extract short-term signal patterns; the GRU layers track how those patterns evolve over time, which is what lets the model flag risk building up rather than just reacting to a single bad reading.
