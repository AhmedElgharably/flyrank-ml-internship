Uber Fare Prediction — From Notebook to Live App

ML internship deliverable at Cellula Technologies.

The problem

Estimate a ride's fare from trip conditions — distance, time of day, weather, traffic, car condition — using historical Uber fare data.

Pipeline

EDA on the fare dataset, then a shared scikit-learn ColumnTransformer + Pipeline (StandardScaler for numeric features, OrdinalEncoder for car condition, OneHotEncoder for weather/traffic). Compared a Linear Regression baseline against a tuned HistGradientBoostingRegressor, and saved the winning pipeline for deployment.
